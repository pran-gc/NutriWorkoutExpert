Privacy Policy

NutriWorkoutExpert · Last updated: 11 July 2026

NutriWorkoutExpert ("the app", "we") helps you track nutrition, workouts, weight and health goals. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it lives, and the controls you have. We designed the app to keep the most sensitive data — your photos — on your device.

Who controls your data

The developer of NutriWorkoutExpert is the data controller. You can reach us at sirpgc@gmail.com. Contact us for any privacy request, including access, correction, export, or deletion.

What we collect and why

  • Account: your email address, used only to sign in and secure your account (via Supabase Auth).
  • Health & fitness data you enter: body stats (sex, birth year, height), weight logs, food logs and macros, water intake, workouts and sets, recipes, favorites, goals and targets. This is stored so your data syncs across your devices.
  • Engagement data derived from your logs: streaks, quests, and badges — all computed from the real actions you log; we do not collect self-reported activity.
  • AI insights: text feedback generated for you (weekly reviews, coach plans, physique feedback), stored so you can revisit it.
  • Notification preferences and device push token (only if you enable notifications), so we can deliver reminders and the "your weekly review is ready" alert.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, location, contacts, or browsing history. We do not sell your data.

Photos — stored on your device only

Your photos are never stored on our servers. Meal photos and progress photos are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us and are not part of your synced account data. If you delete your account, local photos are removed from your device.

Optional AI features and Google Gemini

Two features are powered by Google's Gemini API and are strictly opt-in:

  • Snap-to-log (photograph a meal to estimate it) and Physique compare (compare two progress photos for encouraging feedback).

When you use these features, the photo is sent to Google's Gemini API for the moment of analysis only and is not stored on our servers — only the resulting text is kept. For other AI features (weekly review, coach plans), we send only aggregated numbers (e.g. average calories, session counts) — never your raw logs, email, or identity.

Free-tier caveat: these AI features use Google's free-tier Gemini API. Per Google's terms, content sent to the free tier may be processed by Google to improve their services. You are told this before your first use and must explicitly consent. You can revoke consent at any time in Profile, after which no photos are sent for AI analysis.

Where your data is stored

Account and health data are stored in a Supabase project (hosted on cloud infrastructure) with per-user row-level security, so your rows are only accessible to your authenticated account. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by the hosting provider.

Your rights and controls

  • Export: Profile → Export my data returns a complete JSON copy of all your records.
  • Delete: Profile → Delete account permanently erases your account and all associated data, and wipes local photos from your device. This is irreversible.
  • Change password and reset a forgotten password from the app.
  • Notifications: fully user-controlled — enable/disable by category, set quiet hours, or turn everything off.
  • AI consent: revoke photo-AI consent any time in Profile.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is removed promptly and cascaded across all records. Photos are on-device only and are removed on deletion.

Children

NutriWorkoutExpert is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Health disclaimer

NutriWorkoutExpert is a tracking and general-wellness tool, not a medical device. Its calculations and AI suggestions are estimates and general guidance, not medical, nutritional, or fitness advice. Consult a qualified professional before making significant changes.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, surfaced in the app.
